Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: July 22, 2026

This page explains how the website andredestro.github.io handles privacy and analytics. App-specific privacy policies are available on each app's privacy policy page.

Information Collected on This Website

This website may use Firebase Analytics, provided by Google, if you accept analytics cookies. Analytics helps understand site visits, app download clicks, product link clicks, social link clicks, and whether the app catalog loads correctly.

The website does not ask you to create an account and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, phone number, payment information, or precise location.

Analytics Events

When analytics is enabled, events may include:

  • Page views, recorded automatically by Firebase Analytics
  • Successful or failed loading of the app catalog
  • Clicks on App Store download links
  • Clicks on product store links on the setup and product pages (product name and store)
  • Clicks on the setup page link on product pages
  • Clicks on GitHub and LinkedIn links

Cookies and Local Storage

Analytics cookies are only loaded after you accept analytics on the cookie banner. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage so the site can remember it.

You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of any page. You can also clear this preference through your browser settings.

App Store Campaign Links

App Store links on this website include Apple campaign parameters so App Store Connect can attribute downloads that started from this website. These reports are aggregated by Apple and do not identify individual visitors to this website.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase Analytics, if analytics is accepted
  • Apple App Store and App Store Connect campaign attribution for App Store links
  • Amazon and Mercado Livre, as destinations of affiliate product links on the setup and product pages
  • GitHub Pages for website hosting
