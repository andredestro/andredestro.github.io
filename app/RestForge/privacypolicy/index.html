Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

RestForge ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, or other direct personal identifiers to use RestForge.

Workout data you create (such as workouts, presets, exercises, and settings) is stored on your device.

If you enable iCloud sync, this data is stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple CloudKit.

We do not have direct access to your iCloud account content.

2. Analytics and Diagnostics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve features and reliability.

Analytics events are limited to product interactions and technical metrics, such as timer actions, screen views, premium status (true/false), watch commands, and Live Activity interactions.

We do not intentionally send personal data in analytics events (for example: name, email, phone, precise location, or payment data).

3. Data Usage

  • Track workouts, exercises, and rest times
  • Provide local reminders and notifications
  • Display timer information in widgets and Live Activities
  • Improve app quality and feature decisions using aggregated analytics

4. Notifications

RestForge uses local notifications for rest timers and workout events. These notifications are generated on your device.

5. Apple Watch, Widgets, and Live Activities

If you use Apple Watch features, timer state is exchanged between your iPhone and Watch using Apple WatchConnectivity.

Widgets and Live Activities display workout timer information on your device surfaces (Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and widgets) for app functionality.

6. In-App Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your full payment details.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be processed by service providers that support core app operations, such as analytics and Apple platform services.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics (Google)
  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit)
  • StoreKit (in-app purchases)
  • Apple iOS/watchOS system frameworks

9. Data Security

We rely on Apple and Firebase security controls and reasonable technical measures to protect data processed by the app.

10. Your Control

  • Delete workouts, presets, and exercises anytime
  • Disable notifications in app settings or system settings
  • Disable iCloud sync at the system level
  • Delete the app to remove local data from your device

11. Children’s Privacy

RestForge is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
