Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

ExpiryLog ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information You Create in the App

Data you create in the app (for example: item names, expiration dates, categories, reminders, and optional photos) is stored on your device.

If iCloud sync is enabled (Premium feature), this data is stored in your personal iCloud account through Apple CloudKit.

We do not have direct access to your private iCloud data.

2. Analytics Data We Collect

We use Firebase Analytics to understand app performance and feature usage.

Analytics events may include aggregated or technical usage data, such as:

  • Screen views and feature usage
  • Item workflow actions (create, edit, status change, delete)
  • Notification permission result and notification open events
  • Paywall and purchase/restore outcome events

We do not send personal content like item names, category names, photos, free-text user input, or payment card data in analytics events.

3. Notifications

ExpiryLog uses local notifications generated on your device to remind you about expiration dates.

4. Photos

If you attach photos to items, photos are stored locally and, when iCloud sync is active, in your iCloud account.

5. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not collect or store payment card information.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is only shared with service providers needed to operate the app features described here.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Apple CloudKit (optional iCloud sync)
  • Apple StoreKit (in-app purchases)
  • Google Firebase Analytics (usage analytics)

8. Security

We rely on Apple and Firebase security controls and industry-standard transport protections for the data handled by these services.

9. Your Choices

  • Delete your items at any time
  • Disable notifications in iOS Settings
  • Disable iCloud sync from your Apple settings
  • Delete the app to remove local app data from your device

10. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
