Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

SuppTrack ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

SuppTrack stores the routine data you create in the app (such as supplements, daily logs, reminders, and preferences) on your device.

If you enable iCloud sync (Premium feature), this data is securely stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple CloudKit.

We do not have direct access to your personal iCloud account content.

SuppTrack also uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect limited technical and usage data, such as app interaction events, purchase flow events, device/app instance signals, and crash diagnostics.

2. How Data Is Used

  • Track your supplements and daily logs
  • Provide reminders and notifications
  • Display information within widgets
  • Measure product usage and feature engagement (analytics)
  • Detect, investigate, and fix crashes (diagnostics)

We do not use your data for third-party advertising and do not perform cross-app tracking.

3. Notifications

SuppTrack uses local notifications to remind you to take your supplements. These notifications are generated on your device.

4. In-App Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not collect or store payment card or bank information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Limited data may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, such as Firebase (analytics/crash diagnostics) and Apple services (CloudKit and StoreKit).

6. Third-Party Services

  • Apple CloudKit (optional sync)
  • Apple StoreKit (in-app purchases)
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

7. Data Security

Your data is protected by Apple’s systems on your device and iCloud.

8. Your Control

  • Delete supplements and logs anytime
  • Disable notifications
  • Disable iCloud sync
  • Delete the app to remove data

9. Children's Privacy

SuppTrack does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected here.
