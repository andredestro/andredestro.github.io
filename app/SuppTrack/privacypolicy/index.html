Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

SuppTrack ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

SuppTrack stores the routine data you create in the app (such as supplements, daily logs, reminders, and preferences) on your device.

If you enable iCloud sync (Premium feature), this data is securely stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple CloudKit.

We do not have direct access to your personal iCloud account content.

SuppTrack also uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect limited technical and usage data, such as app interaction events, purchase flow events, device/app instance signals, and crash diagnostics.

SuppTrack uses RevenueCat to validate purchases and maintain Premium entitlements. RevenueCat processes an automatically generated anonymous customer identifier and purchase history received through Apple StoreKit, such as the purchased product and subscription, renewal, expiration, and refund status. SuppTrack does not provide RevenueCat with your name, email address, iCloud identifier, or a custom account identifier.

Supplement names, doses, notes, reminder schedules, and daily intake history are not sent to RevenueCat.

2. How Data Is Used

  • Track your supplements and daily logs
  • Provide reminders and notifications
  • Display information within widgets
  • Measure product usage and feature engagement (analytics)
  • Detect, investigate, and fix crashes (diagnostics)
  • Validate purchases, maintain Premium access, and measure subscription performance

We do not use your data for third-party advertising and do not perform cross-app tracking.

3. Notifications

SuppTrack uses local notifications to remind you to take your supplements. These notifications are generated on your device.

4. In-App Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not collect or store payment card or bank information.

RevenueCat processes an anonymous customer identifier and purchase history as a service provider so that SuppTrack can validate purchases, maintain Premium entitlements, restore purchases, and produce subscription analytics.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Limited data may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, such as Firebase (analytics/crash diagnostics), RevenueCat (purchase validation, entitlements, and subscription analytics), and Apple services (CloudKit and StoreKit). We require service providers to protect data consistently with this policy and applicable requirements.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Apple CloudKit (optional sync)
  • Apple StoreKit (in-app purchases)
  • RevenueCat (purchase validation, Premium entitlements, and subscription analytics)
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

7. Data Security

Data stored on your device and in iCloud is protected by Apple’s systems. Data processed by service providers is protected using their respective security measures.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Routine data remains on your device or in your personal iCloud account until you delete it through the app, Apple’s iCloud controls, or by removing the relevant app data. Technical, analytics, diagnostic, and purchase records are retained by our service providers according to their retention policies and as needed to operate the app, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and maintain purchase and subscription records.

You may contact us at the address below to request information about or deletion of data controlled by us. Because SuppTrack does not have user accounts and uses anonymous identifiers, we may need information available on your device to locate a service record, and in some cases we may be unable to associate an anonymous record with you. Records that must be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, accounting, or purchase-restoration purposes may not be deleted immediately.

9. Your Control

  • Delete supplements and logs anytime
  • Disable notifications
  • Disable iCloud sync
  • Delete the app to remove locally stored app data
  • Contact us to make a privacy or deletion request

10. Children's Privacy

SuppTrack does not knowingly collect data from children.
