Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Novumi ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Most data you create in Novumi (such as trackers, dates, notes, and settings) is stored locally on your device using app group shared storage, so that widgets and Live Activities can access it.

Novumi also uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect limited technical and usage information, such as app interactions, crash logs, and performance diagnostics.

We do not intentionally collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

2. How Data Is Used

The data you enter is used solely to:

  • Display your countdowns and streak trackers
  • Show your trackers in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • Power Live Activities on the Dynamic Island and Lock Screen
  • Send local reminder notifications for your tracked events

Technical and usage data is also used to:

  • Understand feature usage and improve product decisions
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and reliability issues

We do not use collected data for third-party advertising, data brokerage, or sale of personal information.

3. Notifications

Novumi uses local notifications to remind you about upcoming or ongoing tracked events. Notifications are scheduled and generated entirely on your device. No data is sent to external servers to deliver notifications.

4. Widgets and Live Activities

Novumi provides Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen widgets, and Live Activities (Dynamic Island). These features read your tracker data from shared local storage on your device. No data leaves your device to power these features.

5. In-App Purchases

Novumi offers optional Premium features (unlimited trackers, premium themes, advanced widgets, and Live Activities) via in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not collect, process, or store any payment information.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited technical and usage data may be processed by our service providers (such as Google Firebase) strictly to provide analytics and crash reporting services.

7. Third-Party Services

Novumi uses the following third-party and platform services:

  • Firebase Analytics (usage analytics)
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting and diagnostics)
  • StoreKit (in-app purchases)
  • ActivityKit (Live Activities)
  • WidgetKit (Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets)
  • UserNotifications (local notifications)
  • iOS system frameworks

8. Data Security

User-created tracker content is stored locally on your device and protected by iOS system-level security. Limited technical and usage telemetry may be transmitted securely to Firebase for analytics and crash diagnostics.

9. Your Control

  • Delete any tracker at any time from within the app
  • Disable notifications in iOS Settings
  • Delete the app to permanently remove all stored data

10. Children's Privacy

Novumi is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
